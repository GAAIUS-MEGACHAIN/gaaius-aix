  3. POST   /api/tutoring/practice-questions         📍 Generate questions
  4. POST   /api/tutoring/evaluate-answer            📍 Score answer
  5. POST   /api/tutoring/socratic-question          📍 Socratic Q
  6. POST   /api/tutoring/study-plan                 📍 Study path
  7. GET    /api/tutoring/session/{session_id}       📍 Session details
